Implementation of NetScaler Web App Firewall for a banking institution, placed in front of more than
300 websites:

- **Signature-based protections**: known attack patterns blocked before they reach the
  applications.
- **Behavioral protections**: traffic that deviates from the applications' expected behavior is
  detected and stopped, covering attacks that signatures alone would miss.
